Canadian financing insights

Business financing isn't one-size-fits-all.

Different purposes, repayment needs and assets call for different financing approaches. Recent Canadian small-business survey results help explain why.

20%

Requested debt financing

Of surveyed Canadian small enterprises requested debt financing in 2025.

45%

Focused on working capital

Of main intended uses of debt financing were working or operating capital.

76%

Involved collateral

Collateral was required for 76% of surveyed small-business debt financing overall.

Source: Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada, Credit Conditions Survey 2025 (1,812 small enterprises with 1–99 employees). Measures have different denominators and do not represent commercial mortgages specifically.
