# KommercialConnect Features & Benefits

**Commercial and business financing, connected.**

KommercialConnect is the borrower-facing concept for connecting Canadian businesses and commercial property owners with finance brokers. The operating intelligence platform is designed to help brokers, contractors and commercial real estate professionals find relevant project signals, understand who is involved, and decide what to do next.

> **Product status:** The repository contains an MVP scaffold and a synthetic-data dashboard. Features marked **MVP direction** describe the current build target; features marked **Planned** are not yet implemented. Project data and stakeholder details require source verification. The platform does not approve financing or guarantee funding, contracts, appointments or project outcomes.

## Choose your path

### For commercial finance brokers

Find projects earlier, prepare for a more informed sponsor conversation, and keep financing opportunities organized.

- **Project discovery — MVP direction**  
  Review construction and development activity with source, date, stage and confidence context.  
  **Benefit:** Spend less time assembling a project picture from disconnected sources.

- **Stakeholder and company context — MVP direction**  
  Connect project records to developers, owners, contractors and other relevant organizations where information is available.  
  **Benefit:** Prepare outreach around the people and project context that matter.

- **Financing pipeline — MVP direction**  
  Track an opportunity from discovery through sponsor verification, financing discussion, mandate qualification, lender review and outcome.  
  **Benefit:** Keep next steps visible and distinguish a project signal from a confirmed financing need.

- **Evidence-backed briefing — Planned**  
  Build concise project and sponsor summaries with source links, dates and explicit unknowns.  
  **Benefit:** Give a broker a practical starting point for a conversation and internal review.

- **Lender-fit and compliant outreach tools — Planned**  
  Support mandate matching and permission-aware follow-up as these capabilities are designed and validated.  
  **Benefit:** Create a more consistent handoff from research to broker action.

**Best fit:** Commercial mortgage and development-finance teams that actively pursue business and property financing opportunities in British Columbia.

## One project record, different professional outcomes

A project may be relevant to several professionals at different stages. KommercialConnect is intended to help each team apply its own qualification criteria rather than treating one project as a guaranteed lead for everyone.

- **Finance broker:** confirm sponsor, timing and financing need before qualifying a mandate.
- **Contractor:** verify trade fit, scope, timing and whether the work is still open to pursuit.
- **Commercial real estate professional:** verify ownership context and whether a genuine mandate conversation is appropriate.

A permit or planning update is a signal to investigate; it does not by itself confirm a funding need, contract opportunity, available mandate or appointment.

## A practical workflow

1. **Find** a project or business signal within the available coverage.
2. **Verify** its source, date, stage and relevant stakeholder details.
3. **Qualify** it against your segment, territory, mandate or capacity.
4. **Act** through a suitable conversation or follow-up.
5. **Track** the next step and record the outcome.

## Designed for a BC-first launch

The initial market focus is British Columbia, with source coverage to be demonstrated and measured by region and project type. The MVP direction includes a project registry, source provenance, stakeholder relationships, search and filtering, opportunity tracking, organization-level access controls, and CSV workflows.

The product-market-fit work identifies commercial finance brokers, contractors and commercial real estate teams as pilot segments. Adoption, data quality, customer outcomes and willingness to pay remain to be validated; market-size estimates are planning assumptions, not proof of demand.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does KommercialConnect provide financing?
It is designed to connect financing inquiries with finance brokers. Financing assessment and any offer are handled by the relevant financing professionals and lenders.

### Does a project alert mean the project needs financing?
No. Public project activity may help identify a conversation opportunity, but financing need and timing must be confirmed with an authorized participant.

### Are all features available now?
No. This page separates MVP direction from planned capabilities. The current repository is an MVP scaffold and its dashboard demo uses synthetic data.

### Does the platform guarantee a project lead, contract or funding?
No. It supports research and workflow management. It does not guarantee access, appointments, contracts, loan approval or funding.

### Where will the service launch?
British Columbia is the planned first market. Coverage will depend on available, permitted sources and verification.
