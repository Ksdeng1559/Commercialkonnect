commercialKonnect — Website & Lead Origination Product Requirements Document

Version: 3.0 — October 24, 2026
Status: Implementation specification; not a claim of completed deployment
Repository of record: Ksdeng1559/KonstructionComOS → docs/COMMERCIALKONNECT_PRD.md
Scope: Borrower-facing WordPress website, organic/paid/AIO acquisition, progressive financing inquiry capture, lead qualification, broker marketplace handoff, and broker growth path.

Executive decision: The website must do both: capture financing demand from business owners actively seeking funding and support proactive financing opportunity discovery for professionals. Public education and verified indicative comparisons are ungated; contact and consent are requested only when the visitor wants personalized options or an introduction.

1. Product purpose and success definition

commercialKonnect.ca is a Canadian business and commercial financing information and opportunity marketplace. Business owners can understand financing choices and request contact with qualified professionals. Brokers and lenders can receive matched, permissioned enquiries and separately use intelligence and prospecting capabilities. North star: qualified financing conversations per active professional per month. Supporting measures include accepted leads, cost per accepted lead, completed applications, buyer repeat purchases, funded outcomes and contribution margin.

Initial coverage: British Columbia first; Alberta and Ontario expand as verified lender/broker service coverage and content quality permit. Primary borrower intents: business loans/working capital; business lines of credit; commercial mortgages/refinancing; construction/development; equipment; asset-based lending.

4. Homepage slider component — CK-INTAKE-003 (P0)

Goal: A usable high-intent amount selector, not a lead-sale event. Every slider is paired with a synchronized exact-value CAD field; frequent amounts are one-tap presets. Product, amount and province carry into the wizard without being requested twice. Native keyboard, mobile and no-JavaScript fallbacks are required.

Behaviour: Slider, presets, visible amount and manual input stay synchronized. Support exact typing/pasting (e.g. $1,250,000). Reject invalid/negative/zero values. Treat range boundaries as UX defaults, not lender criteria: valid out-of-range manual amounts continue into manual review with a notice and are never silently truncated. Use integer CAD/minor units. If financing type changes, reconfigure slider without discarding a manually entered valid number. Allow “Other / Not sure.” Provide labels, clear feedback, a progress state and 44px+ touch targets. Do not send precise financial inputs to ad pixels.

Form design: Ask only necessary questions, show why they matter, allow editing and back-navigation. Keep non-binding results accessible before identity capture. Do not ask for SIN, full bank details or uploads in P0. A phone verification workflow should be queued promptly; target rapid response with an operating-hours SLA rather than guaranteeing contact.

Product-specific branches

  • Business loans/LOC: trading history, revenue band, purpose, timeline, secured/unsecured interests.
  • Commercial mortgages: property type, value band, existing financing band, refinance/purchase intent and occupancy context.
  • Construction: project stage, approximate budget, land or site context, requested debt amount and timing.
  • Equipment/ABL: asset category and broad equipment, inventory or receivables context.

6. Public financing rates and gating policy

The rate comparison guide is indexable and available without registration. Public content may show verified, attributed, time-stamped indicative ranges, eligibility factors, calculations and rate methodologies. Where a current rate or eligibility basis cannot be substantiated, show “Custom quote required.” Do not compare a nominal rate, factor rate and APR as interchangeable measures. The interactive rate guide (CK-RATE-001) may filter product categories without claiming lender approval. Request contact and sharing permission only for actual broker introductions or personalized quotation requests.

7. Organic SEO, AEO/AIO and content engine

Six interlinked clusters: /business-financing/, /commercial-mortgages/, /construction-financing/, /equipment-financing/ and /asset-based-lending/, /industries/, and /compare-financing/ (including /business-loan-rates-canada/). Maintain separate broker content at /brokers/. The approved 50-page plan is a staged architecture, not a mandate for bulk thin content.

Editorial state: topic/keyword opportunity → sourced research → content brief → AI-assisted draft → licensed/qualified expert review → applicable compliance review → draft QA → publish → monitor → refresh/archive. Every financing claim has provenance, assumptions and review date. Include helpful direct answers, worked examples, original comparisons, internal links and visible methodology. Mark sponsored placements. Monitor Search Console, indexation, organic conversion and sampled AI-answer citations; no guaranteed AI search placement.

8. Paid traffic and broker-led acquisition

Google Search captures high-intent product searches; Meta tests educational vertical-specific financing angles; retargeting is consent-dependent. Test messaging and landing pages separately by industry, product and province. Use verified keywords/CPC from planning tools and approval gates for budget changes. Illustrative monthly testing budget: Google Search $1,500, Meta $750, retargeting $250 and creative/testing $500 (CAD $3,000 total); exclude production/engineering costs. Neither CPL nor return is guaranteed.

Broker-led campaigns select sector, territory, product, approval-ready creative and budget. Planned Explee and Ryze integrations require an API/permissions/account-separation proof before use. Broker-originated leads retain agreed originating-broker attribution subject to consent and contractual requirements; platform-acquired leads may enter a different marketplace allocation pool. Paid, organic, AI-referral, partner and outbound traffic remain separate in reporting.

9. Dual opportunity engines and lead classification

Inbound engine: financing enquiries voluntarily submitted through content, paid, referral or broker-specific landing pages. Proactive engine: LeadSniperAI/business and construction signals, research and decision-maker enrichment; an observed expansion or permit is not evidence of borrowing intent. A prospect only becomes a financing-qualified lead after relevant financing need and interest are confirmed.

States: discovered_signal → unverified_inquiry (inbound only) / researched prospect → contact_verified → financing_interest_confirmed → buy_box_qualified → delivered → accepted | rejected | refunded → downstream outcomes. Keep the provenance and permissions of each source independently.

10. Buyer-first PPL marketplace, packages and credit wallets

Before large-scale acquisition, identify 10–15 prospective lead buyers, compare 3–4 competitors, document buy-boxes and secure trial commitments. Buyer rules include province, specialty, product, deal size, business eligibility, monthly capacity, shared/exclusive preference, acceptance/refund rules and lead prices. The supplied Fuller SOP reports a five-step form, fast qualification and repeat-purchase model; its UK case-study economics are not Canadian performance guarantees.

Commercial model (proposed, unvalidated): SaaS Professional CAD $500/month; Team $1,500/month for up to five seats; Organization $3,000/month for up to 15; Enterprise negotiated. Prepaid lead-wallet packages CAD $1,500, $3,000, $5,000 and $10,000+ may be offered under clear terms. Separate subscription access/AI research credits from purchased lead credits. Deliver a qualified, appropriately permissioned product under specified shared/exclusive conditions before recognizing fulfilled lead revenue. Wallets support pooled organization usage, limits, immutable ledger, credits/reversals and no duplicate charges. Pricing tests—not guaranteed prices—should differentiate business financing leads, premium commercial financing leads and appointments.

12. Data contracts, security and compliance

Proposed logical entities, reconcile with live schema first: assessment_sessions, assessment_answers, assessment_result_snapshots, contact_submissions, consent_events, attribution_events, verification_events, broker_profiles, buyer_buy_boxes, lead_qualification_events, lead_assignments, lead_distributions, lead_purchase_events, billing_events, buyer_disputes, lead_requalification_schedules, appointments and audit_events.

Anonymous sessions need opaque identifiers, server ownership binding, short retention/TTL, idempotent secure submissions and no publicly accessible identity data. Identified enquiries require disclosure of sharing purposes and recipient scope; optional marketing consent is distinct from referral/application permissions. Enforce tenant isolation, RLS, rate-limits, suppression, data deletion/withdrawal processes, encryption and restricted API secrets. Respect CASL, applicable Canadian privacy obligations, provincial finance licensing/advertising and sponsorship transparency. Do not transmit PII or financing answers to third-party advertising pixels.

13. Lead routing and second-use governance

Buyer matching starts only after contact and intent verification, authorized sharing and product/geography/amount eligibility checks. Record assignment, number of recipients, exclusivity, quoted price, wallet transaction, broker response and dispute history. Lead replacements require evidence and must reverse the applicable charge without charging twice.

Requalification review cadence from supplied SOP: day 0 delivery; month 3 review; month 4 new-angle contact; month 6 alternative offer; month 9 final scheduled review. Dates are not permission to send marketing. Each contact requires a currently lawful basis, suppression check, original buyer rights review, and renewed needs/interest before resale to another buyer. Distinguish repeat financing needs from stale or duplicated prospects.

14. Analytics, OODA optimization and performance

Track selector_viewed, financing_type_selected, aggregated slider_changed, preset_selected, assessment_started, step_completed, preliminary_results_viewed, contact_submitted, consent_recorded, contact_verified, interest_confirmed, buyer_matched, lead_delivered, buyer_accepted/rejected, wallet_redeemed, meeting_held and funded with privacy-safe payloads. Persist first-known/last-touch attribution, page, campaign and broker ownership from trusted server rules. Weekly Observe → Orient → Decide → Act reporting recommends top three content/qualification/acquisition improvements with human approval for publishing, spending and external actions.

Scorecard: traffic and indexed pages, SEO/AIO referral, selector engagement, assessment completion, results-to-consent conversion, speed to verified contact, verified interest, accepted leads, cost per accepted lead, broker repeat buying, appointments, funded outcomes, wallet disputes, retention and contribution margin. Distinguish credits purchased from delivered/recognized revenue.

16. Definition of done — mandatory tests

  1. A visitor can learn financing options and view preliminary categories anonymously without giving contact details.
  2. Slider, numeric field and presets stay synchronized via keyboard, mouse/touch and mobile; $75,000 and $12,000,000 entries both work exactly; out-of-range input is not silently clipped.
  3. Product/amount/province and first/last trusted attribution are preserved through the wizard without duplicate prompts.
  4. Product-dependent questions display correctly; server validates, deduplicates and safely expires anonymous sessions.
  5. Contact request and separate marketing/referral permissions are auditable; privacy withdrawal prevents prohibited follow-up.
  6. No lead can be sold on a mere signal or unconfirmed application; only permitted matched/verified leads can be distributed.
  7. Brokerage users never access another organization’s applicant or wallet data; unauthorized changes and client-spoofed campaign ownership fail.
  8. Rates, ranking/sponsorship and case-study claims require review/verification, with stale claims flagged and “Custom quote required” fallback.
  9. No financing responses or identities leak to advertising/analytics; ad spend and publication changes require approval.
  10. End-to-end testing follows source → assessment → opt-in → qualification → buyer matching → acceptance/credit fulfillment → CRM/outcome, including refunds and error paths.

17. Decisions, dependencies and explicit non-goals

Decisions: borrower-first homepage; visible broker secondary channel; single shared funnel; ungated factual rate education; slider-led progressive assessment; independent consent gates; verified-interest lead classification; buyer-first PPL model; WordPress/Astra/Gutenberg with secure Supabase/Atomic CRM boundaries. Dependencies: validated broker supply and buy-boxes, lender rate publication rights, WordPress MCP block support, legal review, identity/tenant integration, compliant analytics and live external-provider capability tests.

Not in scope: guaranteed funding, fictitious live rates, automatic credit adjudication, an unrestricted resale of personal information, automated mass cold outreach, guaranteed AI search ranking, unapproved automatic campaign spending or deployment claims without tests.
