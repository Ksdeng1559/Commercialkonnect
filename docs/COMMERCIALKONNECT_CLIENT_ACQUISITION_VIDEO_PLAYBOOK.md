# CommercialKonnect.ca — Client Acquisition & Video Creative Playbook
**Date:** 2026-10-28 · **Status:** GTM plan + example assets; not launched, not approved for paid publishing

## Objectives and audiences
CommercialKonnect.ca has two complementary acquisition tracks:
1. **Business owners / borrowers:** Help BC, Alberta, and Ontario owners identify appropriate business and commercial property funding routes and request a consultation.
2. **Finance brokers / referral partners:** Generate, qualify, attribute and route consented financing opportunities to suitable participating finance professionals. Do not confuse broker-product subscriptions with borrower acquisition.

**North-star acquisition metric:** cost per *broker-accepted, qualified consultation held*, broken down by financing problem, channel and landing page. Secondary metrics: verified financing intent, booking rate, show rate, broker acceptance, eventual funded transactions, and unsubscribe/complaint rate.

## Positioning and client acquisition strategy
**Core promise:** “Explore commercial mortgages and business financing options; connect with financing professionals who understand your goals.” CommercialKonnect is a discovery/connection platform, **not a direct lender**, and should not imply guaranteed rates, approvals or funding.

## Content-to-consultation funnel
- **Creative:** produce 30-second vertical video with a 0–3-second pain/curiosity hook, 3–12s credible problem, 12–23s financing-path explanation, 23–30s relevant CTA, matching subtitles and explicit ending narration. Produce a second, psychologically distinct hook per segment, not cosmetic variations.
- **Organic:** YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, LinkedIn + supporting OpenSEO article, FAQ and 10-page educational PDF; use audience-native edits and calls to action.
- **Paid test:** publish only after lender/claims, brokerage/license and privacy review; use compliant geo/interest audiences, page-specific destinations and small gated tests.
- **Lead capture:** one matching landing page per pain. Request business identity, location, intended use, rough amount band, timeline, current financing stage, collateral/property if relevant, contact permission and referral consent; collect only necessary personal data.
- **Qualification/routing:** dedupe in Atomic CRM / LeadSniperAI, record lawful basis and source, classify financing intent, human-review broker match, then book/confirm consultation; no claim of lender approval. Route to qualified financing professionals only with appropriate authority/consent.
- **Follow-up:** approved Instagram DM keyword → resource delivery → optional consented assessment and booking. Explee/Harvey outbound is a *separate* compliant B2B prospecting lane, not blanket permission to email commenters. Consent scope and CASL / privacy and applicable provincial licensing conditions apply.
- **Measurement:** event_id and UTM: video_view, 3s_hold, video_50, video_complete, CTA_click, guide_requested, form_started, form_submitted, qualified, booked, held, broker_accepted, application_started, funded, disqualified, suppressed. Avoid counting video views/clicks as finance opportunities.
- **Iteration:** weekly scorecards by `creative_id × hook_id × segment × channel × landing_page`; scale qualified consultations held and broker-accepted results, not raw reach.

- Bank decline **diagnosis**: “Your bank said no. Did they tell you what needs to change?”
- Bank decline **alternative-route curiosity**: “One lender's answer isn't necessarily everyone's answer.”
- Cash flow **paradox**: “Profitable on paper, short on cash?”
- Cash flow **contract trigger**: “You landed the contract. Can you fund the job?”
- Owner-occupier **ownership**: “What would it take to buy the space your business already rents?”
- Owner-occupier **renewal pressure**: “Another commercial rent increase?”
- Developer **site-to-capital**: “You found the site. Where does the construction capital come from?”
- Developer **requirements**: “Why might a promising townhouse project struggle to secure financing?”

## Compliance, evidence and publication gates
1. **$2M / fast approvals:** a broad business funding concept was floated in earlier creative. Do **not** publish “funding up to $2 million” or “fast approvals” unless the *current specific lender product(s)*, referral authorization, underwriting, availability and timing claims are independently documented. A $2M financing range **does not imply** a $2M revolving line of credit.
2. No fabricated approval rates, rates, underwriting exceptions, lender commitments, case studies, loan guarantees or “funds in hours” claims.
3. Review Canadian financial advertising, provincial brokerage / referral/licensing obligations, lender representation, CASL and privacy compliance before paid publication.
4. Approval checklist: factual claim ledger → finance/legal review → scene-by-scene voiceover/caption proof → accessibility/brand QA → working consultation landing page + booking → UTM test → CRM routing/suppression tests → written campaign approval.
5. Repository PMF evidence warns against portraying widespread bank rejection: ISED's survey reported high national full-or-partial approval for businesses that applied. Use decline videos as a **specific segment**, not as population statistics.

## 30-day launch experiment
- **Week 1:** finalize three segment landing pages and required intake/consent; QA the existing HeyGen sessions and source project packages.
- **Week 2:** publish organic bank-decline / cash-flow videos and corresponding PDFs; test DM resource workflows.

- **Week 4:** evaluate cost per qualified consultation held, disqualification reasons, broker acceptance and compliance; double down only on strong cohorts.
