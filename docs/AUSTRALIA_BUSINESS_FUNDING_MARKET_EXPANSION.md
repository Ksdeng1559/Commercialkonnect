# CommercialKonnect — Australia Business Funding Market Expansion
**Child strategy:** [Canadian Financing Lead Marketplace Business Model](./CANADIAN_FINANCING_LEAD_MARKETPLACE_BUSINESS_MODEL.md)  
**Related:** [CommercialKonnect PRD](./COMMERCIALKONNECT_PRD.md) · [Astro 7 Programmatic SEO PRD](./ASTRO7_PROGRAMMATIC_SEO_PRD.md)  
**Version:** 0.1 · **Date:** 2026-11-05 · **Currency:** AUD · **Status:** Research and pilot proposal; not launched or compliance-approved.

> **Decision:** Validate an Australian borrower-consented, partner-accepted business funding introduction pilot, starting with NSW construction working capital. Do **not** begin paid lead resale, broker activity, unreviewed cold outreach, or advertise lending approval promises until Australian legal review, partner contracts, consent flows, and unit economics pass the gates below.

## 1. Strategic thesis and boundaries
CommercialKonnect Australia would be a two-sided origination and intelligence marketplace connecting Australian businesses with suitable **Australian-authorised** lenders and finance professionals. It does **not** lend funds, make credit decisions, promise outcomes or treat public business signals as evidence of borrowing intent.

Build one shared technology platform with jurisdiction-specific consent, disclosure, eligibility, content, provider and billing adapters. Do not merge Australian customer data into Canadian campaign audiences by default.

**Distinct economic products:**
1. Educational discovery and broker directory (fixed-fee listing tested separately).
2. Managed acquisition and qualification services for finance partners.
3. Partner-accepted, borrower-confirmed introductions **only if lawful**.
4. Future broker-intelligence subscriptions and specialist origination mandates, each measured separately.

**Non-goals:** loan balance/AUM described as marketplace TAM; inferred funding intent sold as a lead; guaranteed same-day approvals; indiscriminate mass publication of city pages; unlicensed contingent compensation.

## 2. Evidence register and demand segmentation
The supplied strategic analysis identifies overlapping debt-demand purposes: **cash flow 47%, replacement/upgrade capital 41%, new capital 32%, general growth 31%, survival 27%**. Do not sum these percentages; original Productivity Commission methodology, sample and year require primary-source verification.

The earlier CommercialKonnect discussion used ~**996,203 employing Australian businesses** in June 2026 and the ABS-reported **18.3%** of businesses seeking *debt or equity* in 2024–25 as a modelling benchmark. Applying the rate to the more recent stock yields ~**182,305 annual seeker-equivalents**, **not** an observed annual count of credit applications. A separately cited ~15% SME *debt application* rate describes a different denominator/measure; reconcile before replacing the base rate.

The supplied paper also reports **A$224bn private debt AUM in 2025**, including A$132bn business/corporate and A$92bn CRE, with a forecast A$323bn by 2028. These are **research claims pending verification** and constitute a separate capital-stock/supply discussion, **not** lead-market TAM.

### Demand vector → instrument → intake evidence
| Borrower trigger | Candidate instrument(s) | Evidence and suitability questions |
|---|---|---|
| Receivables/cash conversion gap | LOC, overdraft, debtor/invoice finance | Invoice quality, debtor terms, turnover, expected collections, cash-flow forecast |
| Replacement/upgrade of fleet/plant | Equipment loan/lease, asset finance | Asset quote, useful life, deposit, serviceability, security |
| New capex/organic expansion | Term loan, secured debt, growth finance | Use of funds, operating history, repayment capacity, forecast |
| Seasonal inventory and supplier pressure | LOC, trade finance, short-term loan | Orders, inventory cycle, supplier terms, margins |
| Survival/turnaround | Specialist restructuring/refinance | Existing obligations, arrears, viable turnaround path, independent advice |
| Commercial property/development | CRE loan, construction facility, private credit | LTV, valuation, permits, feasibility, sponsor equity, exit |
| M&A/recapitalisation | Acquisition finance, direct lending, equity | Enterprise financials, purchase economics, management and governance |
| Innovation/export | Grants, R&D incentive, VC, growth debt | Eligibility, stage, project budget, dilution/repayment constraints |

Do not claim that invoice finance is always off-balance-sheet or not debt; product structure and accounting treatment differ. Government programs, amounts and availability must be checked on official Australian sources before publication.

## 3. Segments and launch order
**Geography:** (1) NSW/Sydney; (2) Victoria/Melbourne; (3) Queensland/Brisbane; (4) WA/Perth. South Australia is an exploratory second-wave test given the historically higher finance-seeking rate reported in the earlier research.

**Industries:** construction/trades first; retail/wholesale and hospitality next; transport, manufacturing and property/development after partner coverage is demonstrated.

**Products:** working-capital LOC, business term loans and invoice finance first. Equipment finance after provider coverage; CRE/development and direct lending through specialist human-led channels.

**Buyer target:** Australian lender partner, authorised broker group, invoice financier or asset financier with documented states, products, ticket range, exclusions, capacity, acceptance SLA and compensation permissions. Research candidates include Bizcap, Lumi, Prospa, Valiant and FundTap; **no partnership or terms are established**.

**Product allocation hypothesis:** LOC 30%; term/unsecured 25%; invoice 15%; asset 15%; CRE 10%; development/bridging 5%. These mutually exclusive categories are **planning allocations, not observed demand shares**. Same lead is counted once. Do not sum SAM cuts by product, state and industry; each is a view of the same pool.

**Sensitivity:** Base TAM moves ~A$6.84m per A$50 change in realised lead fee, or ~18,230 eligible equivalents for a 10-point eligibility change. Replace fee assumptions with written partner rate cards or accepted invoices.

### Unit economics and governance
**Contribution per accepted lead = collected fee − traffic/acquisition − enrichment − human qualification − compliance/data handling − partner routing − payment and expected credits/refunds.** Record chargeback/rejection cohort, attribution, buyer concentration, time-to-accept, lead age and cash collected. At A$150 revenue, a 60% contribution target requires variable costs **≤A$60** per accepted lead. This is an unverified target.

Keep billing ledgers separate for subscriptions, managed services, one-off production, accepted leads and legally cleared contingent payments.

## 5. Australian partner and compliance gate
**Phase 0 legal diligence (mandatory):** Australian counsel should determine whether each activity/loan category is subject to Australian Credit Licence, credit-representative, financial-services, mortgage/real-estate, state-based, consumer-credit or other obligations. Genuine business-purpose finance is not a blanket legal exemption. Review advertising representations, referral-fee structure, disclosure, insurance, complaints handling and contracts.

**Privacy & marketing:** assess Privacy Act / Australian Privacy Principles, cross-border disclosure from Australian borrowers to Canadian-operated infrastructure, Spam Act email/SMS consent, opt-outs, Do Not Call rules and provenance. No harvested contacts automatically enrolled in outbound campaigns. Obtain specific, auditable borrower authorisation for intended recipients and uses; retention/deletion and subject requests need operational SOPs.

**Partner onboarding record:** legal entity/ABN, accreditation and licensing as applicable, fit (states/product/amount), named contact, approved claims, consent text, referral economics, rejection definitions, assignment SLA, complaint escalation, quality feedback, security and availability.

**No production routing** until legal approval, partner contract, working consent records, tenant isolation and data-processing controls are verified.

## 6. Architecture and workflow
Keep the core **Supabase PostgreSQL** system of record, with restrictive RLS, tenant boundaries, evidence logging and auditable data access; **no Convex**. Existing repository is a Next.js application; [Astro 7 child PRD](./ASTRO7_PROGRAMMATIC_SEO_PRD.md) proposes a **separate future acquisition frontend**, not a completed migration.

Workflow under **ICM human-reviewed gates**:
1. **OpenSEO:** AU en-AU search-intent clusters by state, city, industry and purpose; save date, source, keyword evidence; do not invent search volume/CPC.
2. **Astro/Sanity (proposed):** high-quality education and differentiated local pages, with Australia-specific finance disclosures, canonical URLs and structured content; no doorway-page factory.
3. **LeadSniperAI:** discover businesses, validate ABNs using lawful ABR data/interfaces, collect source-backed commercial signals. Signal alone ≠ intent.
4. **Inbound consent & Harvey:** borrower opts in; confirm purpose, amount, needed-by date, revenue band, entity, security, receivables and business-purpose classification. Human escalation on adverse/complex situations.
5. **Mission Control:** jurisdiction/product eligibility gate → provider match → human approval → one permissioned partner offer → accepted/rejected status → appointment/funded/declined outcome → bill/credit reconciliation.
6. **Messaging:** Twilio SMS, Resend transactional email and Unipile only where lawful, consented and technically configured.
7. **Analytics:** channel × state × industry × product × partner cohort; distinguish enquiries, verified leads, partner-accepted leads, paid transactions and funded outcomes.

**Suggested additional data fields:** country_code AU, state_code, ABN and verification timestamp, finance_purpose, instrument_preference, consent version/source/recipients, business-purpose attestation, requested_amount_aud, urgency, security_type, receivables_value, enquiry_confidence, partner_mandate_id, human_reviewed_at, lead_acceptance_id, rejection_reason, collected_fee_aud, variable_cost_aud, source_provenance. Minimise storage of sensitive financial records.

## 7. Go-to-market experiments
**Beachhead:** NSW contractor cash-flow guide + genuine funding assessment. Second cohort: Victoria working capital/retail. Sample landing concepts:
- /au/nsw/sydney/construction-working-capital/
- /au/vic/melbourne/business-line-of-credit/
- /au/guides/contractor-payment-gap/
- /au/financing/invoice-finance/

URLs are *proposed*, not live, and Australian canonical-domain strategy, SEO and country targeting must be resolved. Avoid implying a local physical office.

**Hypotheses to test:** visitor→consented enquiry; enquiry→qualified; qualified→partner accepted; accepted→paid; buyer repeat purchase; fully loaded cost per accepted lead; rejection reasons. No traffic or funded loan guarantees.

**90-day pilot**
| Window | Deliverables | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–20 | Counsel review, 10 partner discussions, qualification rubric, pricing interviews, consent + ABN field spec | Two interested authorised buyers and viable lawful transaction structure |
| Days 21–40 | OpenSEO clusters, evidence-backed AU content, two landing pages, localised form, CRM routing | Tested privacy and attribution; no unreviewed production referrals |
| Days 41–70 | Small-budget paid + organic pilot after clearance; human qualification and partner feedback | ≥30 genuine consented enquiries as test target; measured acceptance/rejection |
| Days 71–90 | Reconcile collected revenue, variable costs, repeat buyer demand and consent audits | Positive contribution, two continuing buyers, no unresolved regulatory blockers |

**Stop conditions:** unclear legal basis; no accepted paying buyer; partner rejection systematically high; consent defects; negative contribution with no credible path to improvement; inability to delete/segregate records.

## 8. Institutional/private-credit expansion — separate workstream
The research paper describes rising Australian private-credit AUM and demand in CRE, M&A, refinancing and ABL. Establish a **separate** project-origination registry with lender mandates, sponsor verification, evidence packs, legal due diligence, deal confidentiality and human-authorised introductions. Build TAM by **annual addressable deals × viable referral/origination economics**, not AUM × a fee rate. No guaranteed investment or funding outcomes.
