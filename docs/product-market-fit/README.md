# Product Market Fit

Market sizing, customer segmentation and validation planning for KonstructionComOS / ClearConnect.

## Current analysis

[KonstructionComOS — TAM, SAM and Product-Market Fit](KonstructionComOS_TAM_SAM_Product_Market_Fit.md)

The October 14, 2026 analysis covers:
- Canadian TAM and planned BC SAM, with formulas, source links and sensitivity scenarios.
- Mortgage brokers, contractors and contractor specialties.
- Developers, lenders, commercial real estate teams, property managers, appraisers and advisory/referral professionals.
- Buying triggers, pricing hypotheses, segment workflows and a 90-day validation plan.

Base modeled annual subscription opportunity: CAD $125.2 million national TAM and $12.7 million planned BC SAM. The mortgage-broker planning pool is separate and unverified. These are scenario estimates, not measured spending or revenue.

Product-market fit remains unproven. Paid-pilot participation, repeat usage, attributable opportunities and renewals must validate the customer hypotheses.

## Maintenance

Keep sourced facts, assumptions, proposed features and observed customer evidence distinct. Update the analysis date and sources when revising market inputs. Deduplicate buying organizations before combining segments. Record segment-level pilot outcomes before describing fit as validated.
