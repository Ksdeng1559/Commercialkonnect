# PRD — AI Adoption Grants & Modernization Funding Advisory

Status: Proposed | Date: 2026-11-12 | Owner: CommercialKonnect

## Objective
Add a human-supervised AI adoption funding advisory service to CommercialKonnect. CommercialKonnect originates and qualifies SME modernization opportunities; a professional grant writer evaluates program fit, prepares applications and manages submissions with explicit client authorization. Complement grants with repayable government contributions, BDC/commercial loans, and eligible tax incentives. Never describe loans or repayable contributions as grants.

## Pilot
Biomed International Products Corp. / Nutramedics: illustrative CAD 750,000 modernization budget (CAD 250,000 equipment; CAD 500,000 AI/digital). Validate legal entities, expenditure eligibility, actual quotes, throughput, labour savings, inventory metrics and program status before external claims. The PacifiCan RAII November 23, 2026 deadline and terms cited in preliminary briefing are unverified and must be checked against official sources.

## Roles and approvals
- CommercialKonnect advisor: prospecting, intake, project budget, financing options, lender referrals and client communications.
- Professional grant writer: eligibility diligence, program research, document checklist, application preparation, compliance review, submission and reporting requirements.
- Client: supplies accurate evidence, approves budget and every application/submission.
- Human-in-the-loop: no automatic submission, program eligibility guarantee, or lender commitment.

## MVP workflow
1. LeadSniperAI discovers businesses with modernization signals; CommercialKonnect CRM creates an opportunity.
2. Intake captures business entity, province, industry, employee count, revenue band, years operating, project description, technology readiness, eligible costs, timing, financing need and consent.
3. Funding screening records program name, official source URL, source-check date, intake window, geographic/sector eligibility, eligible costs, funding type (non-repayable grant / repayable contribution / loan / tax incentive), match funding, stacking restrictions, advisor assessment and confidence.
4. Grant writer receives an assigned case and prepares evidence checklist, milestones, narrative and application package.
5. Client reviews and authorizes each submission; writer records submission ID, date, deadline and status.
6. CommercialKonnect runs parallel lender assessment where appropriate, without double counting funding or assuming approval.
7. After decision, track award terms, disbursement, compliance obligations, claims and measurable modernization outcomes.

## CRM data and stages
Entities: organizations, modernization_projects, program_catalog, eligibility_assessments, grant_applications, application_documents, approvals, funding_sources, project_budgets, implementation_kpis, audit_events.
Stages: Discovered → Intake → Eligibility Screening → Qualified → Engagement Signed → Documentation → Writer Review → Client Approval → Submitted → Decision → Disbursement/Compliance → Closed.
Permissions: role-based access, organization-level isolation, encrypted document storage, consent and retention policies; grant writer accesses only assigned cases. Audit all decisions and submissions.

## Offer and commercial validation
Test CAD 750 funding-readiness assessment; CAD 2,500–7,500 application preparation; optional CAD 1,000–3,000 monthly advisory. Pricing is hypothetical, subject to grant-writer subcontract economics and program restrictions on consultant fees or contingency arrangements. Disclose commercial financing referral compensation and conflicts. Do not imply government affiliation.

## Acceptance criteria
- Every recommended program has a dated official-source verification and correct funding classification.
- No application moves to Submitted without grant-writer review and recorded client approval.
- Every financial scenario reconciles funding sources, company equity and eligible expenditure, with stacking constraints flagged.
- Every opportunity has an accountable owner, next action, document checklist and timestamped status.
- Pilot: screen 20 BC SMEs, obtain 5 plausible qualified projects, test 2 paid assessments; targets, not achieved results.
- Report conversion, application completion, approval rate, total awarded versus requested, time-to-decision, service gross margin and client outcomes separately.

## Delivery phases
Phase 1: Service SOP, grant-writer engagement terms, program verification and manual CRM pilot.
Phase 2: Funding assessment form, program catalog, grant-writer workbench and document workflow.
Phase 3: LeadSniperAI signals, automated alerts and lender/implementation cross-sell, all with human approval.

## Exclusions and risks
No guarantee of funding, no fabricated eligibility or deadlines, no autonomous filing, no blanket promise of non-repayable AI adoption grants. Confirm applicable provincial licensing, privacy, anti-spam, referral and compensation requirements before launch.
