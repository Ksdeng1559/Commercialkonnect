# Commercial Finance Experts Landing Page Wireframe

**Brand:** KommercialConnect (proposed borrower-facing brand) / KonstructionComOS (operating intelligence platform)  
**Audience:** Commercial mortgage and development-finance brokers, business-financing brokers/advisors, private lenders and capital advisors  
**Launch:** British Columbia, with the initial service area bounded by demonstrated project-source coverage  
**Status:** Marketing and product concept; the app is an MVP scaffold with synthetic demo data. Features below are build targets unless explicitly available.

## Conversion goal

Show finance experts how the workflow connects project discovery, client preparation and broker-reviewed outreach to capital origination. Invite a demo through the existing, tested route. Do not create a new signup path before the internal-pilot readiness gate is cleared.

**Primary CTA:** Request a broker demo  
**Secondary CTA:** See the workflow  
**Optional CTA:** Discuss a pilot

## Recommended page copy

### Hero

**Know the project. Understand the client. Be ready for the conversation.**

KommercialConnect brings project signals, client context, meeting preparation and broker-reviewed outreach into one commercial-finance workflow. Check where information came from, see what still needs confirmation, and keep each next step connected to the opportunity.

**CTA:** Request a broker demo  
**Supporting note:** British Columbia first. Source coverage and data freshness vary by region and project type.

### Problem

A planning notice or construction update may be worth investigating, but it does not tell you whether the sponsor is seeking capital, who controls the decision, or which financing route could fit. Brokers must assemble project and client context, prepare for the conversation and follow up without losing details between research tools and the pipeline.

### Outreach service copy

**Turn research into a relevant first conversation.**

Use verified project and client context to prepare a short video introduction or tailored email. A broker reviews each message before it is sent. Replies and engagement can help prioritize follow-up, while financing need remains unconfirmed until the prospect says so.

**Benefit:** Move from research to a specific, reviewable introduction and keep the response connected to the opportunity.

**CTA:** See a sample broker-reviewed sequence

### Battlecard preview

A sample battlecard should include:

1. Project and sponsor snapshot with source links and dates.
2. Client-stated goals, timing and financing status.
3. Potential financing approaches labeled as questions or hypotheses.
4. Discovery questions on purpose, timing, amount range, existing obligations, assets/security and decision process.
5. Missing information and mandate-fit checklist.
6. Meeting agenda, owner, next step and follow-up date.

Do not state inferred objections, client intent, creditworthiness or lender appetite as fact.

## Outreach and compliance interaction requirements

- Build from source-backed facts and approved templates; show source and date beside personalization inputs.
- Let the broker edit and approve the recipient, message, disclosure, channel and destination before sending.
- Store evidence of contact eligibility, consent where required, suppression and opt-out; include an unsubscribe for commercial email where required.
- Do not use scraped public contacts for outbound marketing without lawful authorization and suppression handling. Follow CASL and applicable privacy requirements.
- Apply contact-frequency limits and stop follow-up after opt-out or suppression.
- Keep sensitive borrower intake and financial documents off unsecured email or video pages; use approved secure channels.
- Track assignment, delivery, video engagement, reply, CTA, booking, qualification and outcome as separate events. Engagement alone is not qualification.
- Evaluate matched cohorts and report sample sizes. Do not claim response or conversion lift until measured against a suitable baseline.
- Keep provider selection open. Review tenant use, privacy, analytics, security, support and cost before selecting a video provider.

## Visual and interaction direction

- Show the workflow in the hero product preview rather than relying on generic construction photography.
- Separate **Observed**, **Client confirmed**, **Inferred**, and **Needs verification**.
- Show financing status as **Unknown** until confirmed by an authorized participant.
- Mark video and email drafts **Needs broker review**; show no automatic-send state.
- Keep this page focused on brokers; link to separate business-owner and contractor journeys.
- Stack workflow and outreach cards vertically on mobile; keep the primary CTA easy to reach.
- Use accessible contrast, keyboard navigation, descriptive alt text and status labels that do not rely on color alone.
- Use illustrative records only in prototype views and disclose synthetic content.

## CTA form fields

Use the existing tested demo-request route. Suitable fields are name, work email, organization, role, financing focus, regions served, typical deal types and preferred next step. Do not request borrower financial documents on the marketing page.

## Claims to avoid

- Guaranteed funding, approvals, contracts, meetings, exclusive opportunities or response rates.
- “Every project in BC,” superior accuracy or complete coverage without measured evidence.
- Claims that a project signal proves financing intent or a client’s likely needs.
- Specific time savings, revenue uplift, deliverability or campaign conversion before testing.
- Product-market-fit or traction claims before paid-pilot usage and renewal evidence exist.
